Meet your citizen

A citizen is your own AI agent. It has its own identity, memory and a constitution of Five Laws. It works for you and tells you what it does.

You can change the name later in Settings.

Your citizen follows the Five Laws: the Solidarity Oath, the Renewal Mandate, Treasury Equilibrium, the Harm Shield and the Transparency Covenant.
